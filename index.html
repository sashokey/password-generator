Security and sources

Passwords are generated locally with Web Crypto. The app does not store or transmit them. Copying places the password on your system clipboard.

Estimated entropy is the base-2 logarithm of the number of possible outputs. The app’s advisory target is 80 bits. For more entropy, increase the length or choose Random characters.

NIST guidance for password verifiers sets a 15-character minimum for password-only sign-in and allows a minimum of 8 with MFA.

Dictionary sources: WordNet 3.0 (license) and the EFF Long Wordlist by Joseph Bonneau / Electronic Frontier Foundation (CC BY 4.0). Entries were filtered to single words, normalized, and deduplicated. EFF hyphens and dice indices were removed. WordNet nouns were grouped by first sense.
